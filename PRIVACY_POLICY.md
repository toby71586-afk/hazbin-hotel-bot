# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 29, 2026**

## 1. What We Collect
The Hazbin Hotel Welcome Bot ("the Bot") collects and processes the following data:
- **Discord User ID** — used to identify new members and send welcome messages
- **Discord Server ID** — used to determine which channel to send welcomes to

## 2. How We Use Data
- The Bot uses this data solely to send welcome messages when new members join a server.
- No data is stored permanently. User IDs are processed in memory only at the moment a member joins and are not logged or saved.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties.

## 4. Data Retention
No user data is stored. All processing is ephemeral and occurs only at the moment a new member joins.

## 5. Your Rights
Since no data is stored, there is no data to request, modify, or delete. If you have concerns, contact the bot developer.

## 6. Changes
This policy may be updated. Continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of the current policy.

## 7. Contact
For privacy concerns, contact the bot developer through the Hazbin Hotel community.
